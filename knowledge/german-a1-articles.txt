# German A1 — Articles and Nouns

German nouns have grammatical gender.

The definite articles are:
der for masculine nouns,
die for feminine nouns,
das for neuter nouns.

Examples:
der Mann
die Frau
das Kind

The indefinite articles are:
ein for masculine nouns,
eine for feminine nouns,
ein for neuter nouns.

Examples:
ein Mann
eine Frau
ein Kind

German nouns are always written with a capital letter.

Examples:
Haus
Auto
Schule
Familie
Sprache

Plural nouns normally use the article die.

Examples:
die Männer
die Frauen
die Kinder
die Häuser

The article is an important part of a German noun and should normally be learned together with the noun.
